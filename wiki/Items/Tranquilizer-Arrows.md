# Tranquilizer Arrows

Back to: [Items Index](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/items-index)

![Crafting Tranquilizer Arrows](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/storage/mods/019ce94e-972f-717d-8881-7a3d542de0c3/files/aa1ea5e3-71da-4159-b6e8-3dc094e127bc.png)

Tamework 5.1.0 renamed this item to **Tranquilizer Dart**. Tamework 5.0.x still calls it Tranquilizer Arrow. The item id `Weapon_Arrow_Tranquilizer` is unchanged.

## Used For
- Fired from the [Tranquilizer Dart Rifle](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/tranquilizer-shortbow) to tranquilize Beasts for taming.
- Depends on [Tranquilizer Potion](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/tranquilizer-potion), which can be sustained by growing [Glowing Purple Mushroom Spores](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/glowing-purple-mushroom-spores).

## Recipe
- 1x Iron Ingot
- 3x White Feathers
- 1x Tranquilizer Potion
- 2x Plant Fiber

## Bench Requirement
- [Animal Husbandry Workbench](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/animal-husbandry-workbench)

## Earlier Versions
- Animal Husbandry 4.0.0 crafts this item at the Weapon Bench (Bow, Tier 2).
- Tamework 5.1.1 and earlier use 6x Crude Arrows, 1x Tranquilizer Potion, and 2x Plant Fiber.

## Craft Time
- 1 second

## Output
- 6
