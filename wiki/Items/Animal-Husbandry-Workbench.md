# Animal Husbandry Workbench

Parent: [Items Index](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/items-index) | [Home](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/)

The **Animal Husbandry Workbench** is the crafting station for Animal Husbandry gear. It has one tier, and none of its recipes have a tier requirement.

## Recipe
- 6x any wood trunk
- 2x Light Leather
- 20x Plant Fiber

## Bench Requirement
- Workbench (no tier requirement)

## Craft Time
- 3 seconds

## What You Craft Here

| Item | Ingredients | Craft Time |
|---|---|---:|
| [Animal Control Flute](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/animal-control-flute) | 12x any wood, 8x Plant Fiber, 1x Concentrated Life Essence | 4 s |
| [Combat Beast Flute](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/combat-beast-flute) | 10x Wild Wisteria Logs, 2x Shadoweave Fabric Scrap, 1x Concentrated Life Essence, 10x Fire Essence | 5 s |
| [Soul Lantern](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/soul-lantern) | 6x any wood, 2x Void Essence | 3 s |
| [Saddle](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/saddles-and-blankets) | 4x Light Leather, 2x Bolt of Wool | 3 s |
| [Butcher's Knife](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/butchers-knife) | 2x Iron Ingot, 2x Light Leather | 3 s |
| [Herbivore Feed](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/feed-items) (makes 10) | 5x any vegetables, 5x any fruits | 1 s |
| [Carnivore Kibble](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/feed-items) (makes 10) | 10x any meats | 1 s |

| Scarecrow | 1x Concentrated Life Essence, 10x Plant Fiber, 10x any wood | 3 s |
| [Tranquilizer Dart Rifle](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/tranquilizer-shortbow) | See the item page | 4 s |
| [Tranquilizer Darts](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/tranquilizer-arrows) (makes 6) | See the item page | 1 s |
| [Nametag](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/nametag) | 2x Light Leather, 1x Gold Ingot | 1 s |

Notes:
- The Scarecrow is a Tamework item. It pauses automatic spawn markers within 32 blocks and ordinary mob spawns in the chunks that area touches. Break it to pick it back up.
- The Nametag recipe appears here only when Alec's Nametags 1.3.0 or later is installed.
- In Animal Husbandry 4.0.0, the Tranquilizer Dart Rifle and Tranquilizer Darts are still crafted at the Weapon Bench (Tier 2). Later versions move both recipes to this workbench.
