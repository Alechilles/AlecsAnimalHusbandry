# Eggzooka

Parent: [Items Index](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/items-index) | [Home](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/)

The **Eggzooka** is an egg launcher. It has no crafting recipe.

## How To Get It
- [Lil Cluck](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/lil-cluck) drops one Eggzooka and 6-10 eggs when killed.

## How To Use
1. Carry eggs in your inventory. The Eggzooka holds up to 3 loaded eggs.
2. Press primary use (left-click) with an empty Eggzooka, or press Ability 3 (`R` by default), to load. It loads one egg every 0.7 seconds until it is full, you run out of eggs, or you fire.
3. Press primary use to fire one egg. You can fire again after 0.5 seconds.

The hopper on the model shows how many eggs are loaded. Switching to another item returns the loaded eggs to your inventory.

## Effects
- Eggs splat where they land.
- A direct hit applies **Egged** for 5 seconds. The target is splotched with yolk, and a player who is hit sees yolk drip down the screen.
- A direct hit deals 4 damage in Animal Husbandry 4.0.0. Later versions raise it to 15.
