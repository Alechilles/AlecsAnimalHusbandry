# Flightmaster's Talisman

Parent: [Items Index](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/items-index) | [Animal Husbandry Wiki](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/)

The Flightmaster's Talisman is a tool from Alec's Tamework. You must hold it to mount and fly a tamed Frost Dragon that you own.

## Craft the Talisman

Craft the Flightmaster's Talisman at an Animal Husbandry Workbench using:

- 10 Gold Ingots
- 3 Zephyr gems

Crafting takes 4 seconds.

If HyDragon is installed, its Draconic Altar also offers this recipe.

## Mount a Frost Dragon

1. Tame and own a Frost Dragon.
2. Put the Flightmaster's Talisman in your hotbar and select it.
3. Hold crouch and press `F` (Interact) on the Frost Dragon.

You cannot mount the Frost Dragon without the talisman selected. The game shows a notice if you try to crouch-interact without it.

## Flight Controls

After you mount, keep the talisman selected.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Hold crouch on the ground, then release | Charge and release a takeoff launch. A longer hold gives a stronger launch. |
| Move forward | Start or continue gliding. |
| Mouse look | Steer and change your climb or dive angle. |
| Left-click | Flap upward. It can also start flight. |
| Right-click | Airbrake. |
| `Q` | Boost forward. It can also start flight. |
| Crouch while airborne | Move downward. |
| `F` | Dismount immediately. |

Launches, flaps, and boosts use Vigour. Land and wait for Vigour to recover if these actions stop working.

## Frost Dragon Abilities

The Frost Dragon adds two talisman abilities. In the default key layout, use `E` for Frost Bolt and `R` for Freezing Breath. These use Hytale's remappable Ability 2 and Ability 3 bindings, so the keys can differ if you changed your controls.

## Flight Tips

- Start on open, level ground so you have room to launch.
- Dive to gain speed, then pull up to trade speed for height.
- Do not enter water while flying; custom flight ends and normal swimming starts.
- Do not switch away from the talisman during the ride. The flight system keeps it selected until you dismount.
