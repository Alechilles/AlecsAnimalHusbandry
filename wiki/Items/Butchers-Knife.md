# Butcher's Knife

Parent: [Items Index](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/items-index) | [Home](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/)

The **Butcher's Knife** culls an owned, tamed animal without opening the command flute panel.

## Recipe
- 2x Iron Ingot
- 2x Light Leather

## Bench Requirement
- [Animal Husbandry Workbench](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/animal-husbandry-workbench)

## Craft Time
- 3 seconds

## How To Use
1. Hold the Butcher's Knife.
2. Aim at a tamed animal you own.
3. Hold primary use (left-click) for 0.8 seconds. A progress indicator fills while you hold.

## Notes
- Max stack: 1.
- Slaughter yield follows the animal's adult stage. See [Adult Aging](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/breeding-and-growth-guide#adult-aging).
