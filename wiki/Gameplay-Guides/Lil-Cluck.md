# Lil Cluck

Parent: [Gameplay Guides Index](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/gameplay-guides-index) | [Home](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/)

Lil Cluck is an optional boss: an oversized chick with sunglasses and a gold chain. He does not spawn naturally.

## How To Summon
1. Tame a chicken chick and make sure you own it.
2. Hold a Gold Ingot.
3. Crouch and interact with the chick. The prompt reads `Offer gold`.

The chick consumes one Gold Ingot and becomes Lil Cluck. He is hostile and is no longer your companion.

Server administrators can also use `/npc spawn AH_Chicken_Chick_Boss`.

## Fight Overview
- Lil Cluck has 1500 health.
- Players within 40 blocks see his boss health bar during combat.
- He does not regenerate health while in combat. Regeneration returns when he leaves combat.

| Attack | Available | What Happens | How To Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peck combo | Always | Three pursuing pecks in a row, each with a short wind-up. | Keep moving away between pecks. |
| Charge | Always | He runs at you. Hitting a wall stuns him for about 2 seconds. | Stand in front of a wall and step aside. |
| Egg Drop | Always | A volley of three marked eggs. Eggs burst into feathers or hatch chicks that chase and peck you. | Leave the marked spots. He cannot use Egg Drop twice in a row. |
| Leap | At 75% health or lower | After a 1-second crouch he leaps onto the position you had at takeoff and releases an expanding ring of feathers on landing. | Move after he takes off, then jump the ring. 8-second cooldown. |
| Peep Show | At 50% health or lower | He lowers his sunglasses for a 1-second warning, then sweeps a red eye laser for 10 seconds. | Run sideways to outpace the beam, or break line of sight behind terrain. 10-second cooldown after firing. |

Hatched chicks stay until you kill them.

## Rewards
- 1x [Eggzooka](/mod/alecs-animal-husbandry/eggzooka)
- 6-10 eggs
